Did it work, and why are we doing it?

Companies remember what they decided, but rarely check whether it worked or whether the reasons behind it still hold. Splat connects decisions, their assumptions, the results they promised and the work they created in one graph, and answers from it with sources.

Why a graph

Every answer is a chain, not a passage of text.

Remove FalkorDB and none of these answers exist.

  • Any depth. Everything that relies on an assumption, followed as far as it goes with one variable-length pattern.
  • PageRank. Untested assumptions ranked by how much work rests on them.
  • Connected components. Proof that two teams building the same thing share no chain of conversation.
  • Proof of absence. Work with no path to a live goal, and "not in the graph" instead of a guess.
Safe by design

Answers run fixed, read-only Cypher. A language model may pick a tool and phrase a summary, but every id and number it writes is checked against the graph. New messages are read into the graph, and fixes are applied, only after they are tried on a GRAPH.COPY sandbox and a person approves.
