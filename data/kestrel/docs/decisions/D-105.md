# Switch email delivery provider

Context: About 2% of invoice emails never arrive, and customers notice.
Decision: Switch email delivery provider.
Project: Email delivery provider switch.
Why: Most failed invoice deliveries are bounces caused by the email provider.
Options considered and not chosen: Negotiate higher limits with the current provider; Send from two providers.
Risks: sender reputation resets on a new provider.
Success means invoice delivery success changes by +1.5% from its pre-launch level by 2026-07-20. Owner of the result: Neha Moreau.
