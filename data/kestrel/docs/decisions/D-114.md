# Shorten the signup form to three fields

Context: Half of visitors who start the signup form abandon it.
Decision: Shorten the signup form to three fields.
Project: Signup flow redesign.
Why: A shorter signup form converts more visitors.
Options considered and not chosen: Sign in with Google only; Remove the company size question.
Risks: sales loses qualifying data.
