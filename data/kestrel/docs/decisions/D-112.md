# Retry failed webhooks with exponential backoff

Context: Support sees a wave of 'payment not recorded' tickets after every partner outage.
Decision: Retry failed webhooks with exponential backoff.
Project: Webhook retry library.
Why: Most support tickets come from failed webhook deliveries.
Options considered and not chosen: Ask partners to poll instead; Add a manual replay button.
Risks: retries could hammer a partner that is already down.
Success means support tickets per week changes by -20.0% from its pre-launch level by 2026-05-25. Owner of the result: Neha Moreau.
