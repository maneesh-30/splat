# Offer a 20% annual plan discount

Context: Monthly churn is 3.1%, well above the 2% goal.
Decision: Offer a 20% annual plan discount.
Project: Annual plan pricing.
Why: Price is the main reason customers churn.
Options considered and not chosen: Lower the entry plan price; Add a pause option instead of cancel.
Risks: discounting trains customers to wait for deals.
Success means monthly churn changes by -15.0% from its pre-launch level by 2026-08-31. Owner of the result: Jonas Kapoor.
