# Add one-click card payments to invoices

Context: Invoices take 21 days to get paid on average; bank transfer is the only option.
Decision: Add one-click card payments to invoices.
Project: One-click card payments.
Why: Customers pay late because paying is inconvenient.
Options considered and not chosen: Partner with a payments aggregator; Offer early-payment discounts.
Risks: card fees eat into small invoices.
Success means average days until an invoice is paid changes by -20.0% from its pre-launch level by 2026-03-09. Owner of the result: Neha Moreau.
