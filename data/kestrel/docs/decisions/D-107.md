# Launch a guided onboarding checklist

Context: Accounts that never connect a bank account churn at three times the average rate.
Decision: Launch a guided onboarding checklist.
Project: Guided onboarding checklist.
Why: Customers churn because they never finish account setup.
Options considered and not chosen: Onboarding calls for every new account; In-app tooltips only.
Risks: a checklist can feel like homework.
Success means monthly churn changes by -10.0% from its pre-launch level by 2026-09-07. Owner of the result: Hugo Sharma.
