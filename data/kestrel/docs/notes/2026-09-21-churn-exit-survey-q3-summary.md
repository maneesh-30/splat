# Churn exit survey, Q3 summary

Exit survey, 212 responses from accounts that churned between June and August. Price is the top reason for only 14% of them. Missing integrations (41%) and setup friction (27%) rank well above it. Recommendation: stop treating churn as a pricing problem.
